About

I am an architect based in Cesate, and I work mainly in the Metropolitan City of Milan.

I work across the whole of architecture: from interiors to new buildings, from housing to retail. When I design I look for sustainability: for the environment and for the people who live in the spaces, in cost and in care over time too. I favour natural materials and spaces that bring out and enhance daylight, through harmony or through contrast.

Every project is an experience that cannot be repeated, one that makes us reflect on the way we live our lives and relate to other people. This is why I believe in architectural design as an act of care and reflection, as well as an exercise in technique and construction.
