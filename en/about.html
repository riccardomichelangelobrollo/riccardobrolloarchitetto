About

I am a young architect, based in Cesate, near Milan. I work across the whole field of architecture: from interiors to new buildings, from homes to retail.

I work in Cesate and the towns around it, from Garbagnate Milanese, Arese and Bollate to Lainate, Senago and Rho, and in Milan.

I try to make every project sustainable, in the materials I choose and in the social life it makes possible.

I believe architecture can change the way we live and the way we relate to one another, and that it should bring people together. I look for truth in materials, favouring natural ones, and for solutions that let daylight lead.
