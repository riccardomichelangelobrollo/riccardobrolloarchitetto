Casa Blocco

Location
Legnano
Year
2025
In collaboration with
Arch. Giuseppe Brollo

In this project we chose to put daylight at the centre, freeing the space from its existing partitions and entirely rethinking the interior layout.

On entering, the eye immediately meets a rounded volume clad in Marazzi three-dimensional tiles, which reinterpret the irregularity and material quality of handmade ceramics. The volume, which hides a storeroom inside, becomes the protagonist of the living area and acts as a kind of traffic warden: it directs the flows, organises the routes and mediates between the night and day areas, while separating the entrance, the bathroom and the bedrooms.
